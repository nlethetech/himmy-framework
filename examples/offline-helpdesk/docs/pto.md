# Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.
**Part-time** employees accrue PTO **prorated** by their scheduled hours.

Unused PTO rolls over up to **5 days** into the next year.
Sick leave is tracked **separately** and does not count against PTO.
Request time off at least **two weeks** in advance through the HR portal.
